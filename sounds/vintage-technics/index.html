What Gateron Yellows sound like

Gateron is a Chinese maker of MX-compatible switches, popular with people who build their own keyboards. The Yellow is a linear switch, slightly heavier than a Cherry MX Red, and a popular budget choice. Like any linear, it has no bump or click, so its sound is the stem bottoming out and returning.

“Black bottom” means Yellows with black bottom housings, a variant some builders seek out for its sound. Swapping on Cherry MX top housings and lubing the sliders are common mods. Lube smooths the travel and takes away scratch, which is why modded linears like these are often described as muted or “creamy”.

About the board

The uploader describes it as a “Hengyu Computer Equipment Factory” Technics keyboard and lists the model as unidentified. They also wrote that its layout is terrible, and that they're “kinda mad at how nice it sounds.” The recording is 51 seconds of typing, saved as stereo Ogg Vorbis.

Short as it is, it filled every band with six regular-key takes, plus 2–4 per band for the wide keys and 6 key-up takes. In the app this pack is listed as Vintage Technics.

Who it suits

Pick Gateron Yellow if you like the sound of a lubed custom board: smooth, low and short, with little ring.

Pick something else if you want a crisp, bright linear (MX Red) or a click (MX Blue).

Gateron Yellow (Vintage Technics): questions

Are these real Gateron Yellow switches?

Yes, per the uploader: “black bottom gat yellows with MX tops swapped on”. The bottom housings are Gateron Yellow and the top housings were swapped for MX tops, and the sliders are lubed, so a stock Yellow may sound a little different.

What does lubing do to the sound?

It smooths the stem's travel and damps scratch and ring. These sliders were wax-lubed and lubed with Krytox 205g0. In this pack the medium takes fall 30 dB below their peak in 49 ms, the shortest of the 15 packs.

Why is the pack called Vintage Technics?

Because the keyboard is the starting point: an old Technics-branded board the uploader couldn't identify. The Gateron Yellows are a mod inside it, which is why this page leads with the switch.
