MacBook typing sound app: what works on which MacBook

Every model below plays all 15 sound packs in Keyslam, the keyboard sound app for Mac. The difference is force sensing, which needs the motion sensor that Apple builds into newer Apple silicon MacBooks. Model families are from Apple's model guides; sensor support comes from public hardware information, and we test on a 14-inch MacBook Pro with M5 Pro.

MacBook Air

The M2 MacBook Air was the first Air with the sensor; iFixit found it in its 2022 teardown. Public reports list it in every Air since; the 2020 M1 Air doesn't have it.

Built-in keyboard, external keyboard, desk or lap

  • Built-in keyboard: force sensing feels each key through the MacBook's case. How force sensing works.
  • External keyboard: sounds play for any keyboard. The sensor can't feel keys that aren't on the MacBook, so they play at your usual strength rather than dropping to soft.
  • Desk, lap or sofa: the reference adapts to your recent typing, so a softer surface doesn't leave everything stuck on soft.
  • Speakers or headphones: Keyslam plays through your current output, including AirPods (Bluetooth adds its own delay). With headphones, only you hear it.

MacBook questions

Why doesn't my MacBook keyboard click?

MacBook keys are built to be quiet: short travel and no click mechanism in the switch, so most of what you hear is a soft tap. Keyslam adds a real recorded click, clack or thock through your speakers or headphones. For what macOS itself offers (and doesn't), see how to turn Mac keyboard click sounds on or off.

Does Keyslam work on a MacBook Air?

Yes, on every MacBook Air that runs macOS 13 Ventura or later. On MacBook Air models with M2, M3, M4 or M5 it also feels how hard you type. The M1 MacBook Air (2020) has no motion sensor, so you pick the strength; see keyboard sounds on the M1 MacBook Air and Intel Macs.

Does force sensing still work with my MacBook on my lap?

It's designed to. Keyslam judges each keystroke against your own recent typing, the median of your last 96 felt keystrokes, so it re-learns what “medium” means when you move from a desk to your lap or a sofa. Give it about a hundred keystrokes to settle.

Which pack sounds most like a MacBook keyboard?

The Laptop pack. It is cut from a close-miked recording of a 2018 MacBook Pro's butterfly keyboard, so it sounds like a MacBook. Newer MacBooks use scissor keys, so it may not match yours exactly. Hear the MacBook keyboard sound test.
